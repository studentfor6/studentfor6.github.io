  <h2 class="text-xl font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-3">Purpose</h2>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-4">To expedite the approval &amp; availability of safe and effective medicines authorised by trusted regulators (Reference Regulators – see list below) to patients in the UK, while maintaining MHRA’s independent oversight.</p>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">Reference Regulators (RRs)</h3>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1 text-base text-slate-700 mb-4">
    <li>Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), Australia</li>
    <li>Health Canada, Canada</li>
    <li>SwissMedic, Switzerland</li>
    <li>Health Science Authority (HSA), Singapore</li>
    <li>Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency (PMDA), Japan</li>
    <li>Food and Drug Administration (FDA), United States</li>
    <li>European Medicines Agency (EMA)</li>
    <li>Member State Competent Authorities of the EU, Norway, Iceland and Lichtenstein</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">Evolution Timeline</h3>

  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2"><strong>CHM</strong> – Commission on Human Medicines</p>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">Eligibility &amp; submission</h3>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">Both IRP routes are substantially shorter than the 210-calendar day timetable for a national MA application.</p>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">Suitability for Recognition A or B is determined by means of an online eligibility form to be completed by the applicant 6 weeks before the planned date of MAA submission.</p>

  <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">When and how to submit the eligibility form?</h4>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">Post-authorisation IRP applications: variations and renewals</h3>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">Applicable for line extensions, variations (Type 1B, Type II) and renewals. Type 1A variations may be submitted if approved by RR grouped with Type 1B or Type II variations.</p>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">Applicable for products that have been initially authorised or subsequently varied via standalone national, MRDCRP or ECDRP routes. IRP route can be availed after RR approval. Applications should be submitted to MHRA as soon as possible, and no later than 60 days following the final assessment being available from RR.</p>
  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">It is generally recommended to use the same RR for IRP applications throughout the product lifecycle, unless justified on patient benefit grounds. Recognition routes A and B are only applicable for new IRP MAAs and line extensions.</p>

  <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">For new IRP MAAs, the submission should include:</h4>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1 text-base text-slate-700 mb-4">
    <li>Documentation of the RR’s approval decision</li>
    <li>All available iterations of the RR’s assessment reports for the initial authorisation and any major post-authorisation procedures (for example, significant variations, renewals)</li>
    <li>The final product information (or international equivalent) approved by the RR</li>
    <li>A list of differences (other than formatting) between the dossier approved by the RR and that submitted to MHRA</li>
  </ul>

  <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">For post-authorisation IRP applications (including variations), the submission should include:</h4>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1 text-base text-slate-700 mb-4">
    <li>Documentation of the RR’s approval decision</li>
    <li>All iterations of the RR’s assessment reports for the relevant post-authorisation procedure - note that an RR assessment report may not be needed for all Type IB variations, but should be provided where available</li>
    <li>The final product information (or international equivalent) and/or updated risk management plan approved by the RR (if applicable)</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="text-base text-slate-700 mb-2">The complete list of RR documents for new IRP MAA is available at International Recognition Procedure - GOV.UK.</p>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-900 mb-2">Key Takeaways</h3>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1 text-base text-slate-700 mb-4">
    <li>IRP offers the fastest UK route when leveraging EMA or other trusted RR approvals.</li>
    <li>Recognition A (60 days) is ideal for straightforward cases; Recognition B (110 days) covers complex scenarios.</li>
    <li>MHRA conducts targeted assessment; retains discretion to reject.</li>
    <li>UK-wide licensing under Windsor Framework simplifies lifecycle management.</li>
    <li>Strategic use of IRP enables synchronised EU–UK submissions, strengthening regulatory agility post-Brexit.</li>
  </ul>
