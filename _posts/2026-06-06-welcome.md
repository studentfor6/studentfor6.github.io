<div class="prose prose-sm max-w-full">
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">MHRA International Recognition Procedure (IRP)</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Purpose</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">To expedite the approval &amp; availability of safe and effective medicines authorised by trusted regulators (Reference Regulators – see list below) to patients in the UK, while maintaining MHRA’s independent oversight.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Reference Regulators (RRs) –</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), Australia</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Health Canada, Canada</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">SwissMedic, Switzerland</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Health Science Authority (HSA), Singapore</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency (PMDA), Japan</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Food and Drug Administration (FDA), United States</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">European Medicines Agency (EMA)</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Member State Competent Authorities of the EU, Norway, Iceland and Lichtenstein</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Evolution Timeline</p>
</div>

<div class="prose prose-sm max-w-full mt-4">
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">CHM – Commission on Human Medicines</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Both IRP routes are substantially shorter than the 210-calendar day timetable for a national MA application.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Suitability for Recognition A or B is determined by means of an online&nbsp;eligibility form&nbsp;to be completed by the applicant 6 weeks before the planned date of&nbsp;MAA&nbsp;submission.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">When and how to submit the eligibility form?</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Post-authorisation&nbsp;IRP&nbsp;applications: variations and renewals</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Applicable for line extensions, variations (Type 1B, Type II) and renewals. Type 1A variations may be submitted if approved by RR grouped with Type 1B or Type II variations.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Applicable for products that have been initially authorised or subsequently varied via standalone national, MRDCRP or ECDRP routes.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">IRP route can be availed after RR approval.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Should submit the application to MHRA as soon as possible, and no later than 60 days following the final assessment being available from RR.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Generally recommended to use same&nbsp;RR&nbsp;for&nbsp;IRP&nbsp;applications throughout the product lifecycle, unless justified on patient benefit grounds.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Recognition routes A and B is only applicable for new&nbsp;IRP&nbsp;MAAs&nbsp;and line extensions.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">For new&nbsp;IRP&nbsp;MAAs, the submission should include:</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Documentation of the&nbsp;RR’s approval decision</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">All available iterations of the&nbsp;RR’s assessment reports for the initial authorisation and any major post-authorisation procedures (for example, significant variations, renewals)</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">The final product information (or international equivalent) approved by the&nbsp;RR</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">A list of differences (other than formatting) between the dossier approved by the&nbsp;RR&nbsp;and that submitted to MHRA</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">For post-authorisation&nbsp;IRP&nbsp;applications (including variations), the submission should include:</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Documentation of the&nbsp;RR’s approval decision</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">All iterations of the&nbsp;RR’s assessment reports for the relevant post-authorisation procedure - note that an&nbsp;RR&nbsp;assessment report may not be needed for all Type IB variations, but provided where available</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">The final product information (or international equivalent) and/or updated risk management plan approved by the&nbsp;RR&nbsp;(if applicable)</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">The complete list of RR documents for new IRP MAA is available at International Recognition Procedure - GOV.UK.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Key Takeaways</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">IRP offers the fastest UK route when leveraging EMA or other trusted RR approvals.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Recognition A (60 days) is ideal for straightforward cases; Recognition B (110 days) covers complex scenarios.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">MHRA conducts targeted assessment; retains discretion to reject.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">UK-wide licensing under Windsor Framework simplifies lifecycle management.</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-slate-700 mb-2">Strategic use of IRP enables synchronised EU–UK submissions, strengthening regulatory agility post-Brexit.</p>
