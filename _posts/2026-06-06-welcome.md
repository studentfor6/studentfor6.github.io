        <th class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-left font-semibold">Route</th>
        <th class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-left font-semibold">Timeline</th>
        <th class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-left font-semibold">Conditions</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr class="bg-slate-50">
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 font-semibold text-slate-900">Recognition A</td>
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-slate-700">60 calendar days from validation</td>
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-slate-700">
          <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2">
            <li>RR approval within previous 2 years.</li>
            <li>Manufacturing process must match the RR-approved process, with GMP compliance evidence.</li>
            <li>Open to applications that meet IRP criteria and do not meet any of the Recognition B criteria.</li>
            <li>No provision for clock stop.</li>
            <li>If major objections are identified which cannot be resolved within 60 days, the timetable may revert to Recognition B.</li>
          </ul>
        </td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 font-semibold text-slate-900">Recognition B</td>
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-slate-700">110 calendar days from validation</td>
        <td class="border-b border-slate-200 px-5 py-4 text-slate-700">
          <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2">
            <li>RR approval within previous 10 years.<sup><a class="text-[#0f4d8a] hover:underline" href="#fn1">1</a></sup></li>
            <li>Adopted if any of the criteria listed at <a class="text-[#0f4d8a] hover:underline" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/international-recognition-procedure/international-recognition-procedure?utm_source=copilot.com">International Recognition Procedure - GOV.UK</a> are met, e.g.:</li>
            <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2">
              <li>Additional manufacturing sites are cited that have not been assessed by the RR.</li>
              <li>Substantial changes in the manufacturing process or analytical methods compared to that assessed by the RR.</li>
              <li>At least one manufacturing site is not yet GMP certified.</li>
              <li>The product contains a first-in-class new active substance.</li>
              <li>The pivotal clinical data are from single arm studies.</li>
              <li>The pivotal clinical data include real-world data.</li>
              <li>Advanced Therapy Medicinal Products (ATMPs).</li>
              <li>Application for orphan drug designation.</li>
              <li>Comparator product used in bioequivalence or therapeutic equivalence study was sourced outside the UK/EU/EEA (generic/hybrid applications).</li>
              <li>An approved body or notified body report is not available for integral medical device components.</li>
              <li>Proposed container closure system, shelf life or storage conditions differ from those accepted by the RR and/or additional stability studies have been provided to MHRA.</li>
            </ul>
            <li>One clock stop at day 70, with up to 60 calendar days for the applicant to respond.<sup><a class="text-[#0f4d8a] hover:underline" href="#fn2">2</a></sup></li>
            <li>If there are major outstanding objections at day 110, formal advice on approvability is sought from CHM, and the timetable will revert to the national 210-day timetable.</li>
          </ul>
        </td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>

<p id="fn1" class="mt-4 text-sm text-slate-600"><strong>Note 1:</strong> In exceptional circumstances, approvals older than 10 years may be acceptable, e.g. in case of supply shortages for certain products.</p>
<p id="fn2" class="text-sm text-slate-600"><strong>Note 2:</strong> The applicant can request an extension of the clock-stop.</p>
