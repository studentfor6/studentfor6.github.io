## IRP Recognition Routes

**Recognition A** — 60 calendar days from validation; RR approval within previous 2 years; manufacturing process must match RR-approved process.

**Recognition B** — 110 calendar days from validation; used for more complex scenarios; one clock stop at day 70 with up to 60 days for response[^2]

## Key Takeaways

- IRP is the fastest UK route when leveraging trusted regulator approvals
- Recognition A suits straightforward cases; Recognition B covers complex scenarios
- MHRA retains discretion; ensure dossier alignment with RR-approved materials
